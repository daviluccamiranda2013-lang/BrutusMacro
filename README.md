<h1 align="center">Brutus Macro</h1>

<p align="center">
  Macro for Brutus RNG on Roblox. Detects biomes, includes anti-AFK, and sends notifications to Discord.
</p>

<p align="center">
  <img src="Icon.png" alt="Brutus Macro" width="220">
</p>

• ⚠️ The macro does not give any kind of warning or ban in roblox!!

Features:

• Detects biomes in the game and sends them to Discord webhooks

• Anti-AFK system that protects you from being disconnected

• Auto-Craft Recorder — record mouse actions to craft potions

PS: This macro is in beta! Everything may change.
